# Customisation

<FeatureTags>
  <FeatureLifecycleStage stage="beta" heading={false} />
  <AvailableSinceVersion version="0.17.0" heading={false} />
</FeatureTags>

Admins can brand DQX Studio for their company under **Settings → Customisation**. It applies to every Studio user. Users still choose light or dark mode themselves.

## Branding

- **Company name** adds your company to the top bar: **DQX Studio | Acme**. It saves as you type. Reset it to show **DQX Studio** only.
- **Logo** replaces the DQX icon in the top bar. Click a logo preview, or drop an image onto it, to upload. The previews show the logo on the light and dark mode top bars. Turn on **Separate logos for light and dark mode** to upload one for each. With separate logos and no dark mode logo, dark mode shows the light mode logo.
- Logos must be PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 256 KB. SVG isn't supported. Logos are shown 24 px tall and up to 160 px wide, so a logo about 200 px tall is plenty.

## Theme

Pick a preset or set five colours: **Brand** (top bar), **Sidebar**, **Page background**, **Interactive elements** (buttons and highlights) and **Text**. Studio derives the rest (cards, hover, borders, button text) from them.

- Use **Light** and **Dark** under the preview to choose which mode's colours you are editing. The preview always shows both modes.
- Changing a colour in one mode generates the same colour in the other mode, until you set that one yourself.
- Saving changes to a preset adds them as a new preset, **Custom 1**, **Custom 2** and so on. Delete a custom preset with its bin icon; the colours in use stay as they are. Saved custom presets are kept when you reset all styling.
- If a colour combination may be hard to read, Studio shows a warning that names the colour to adjust. You can still save.
- Status colours (pass, warn, fail) and chart colours don't change.
- **Reset all styling to DQX Default** removes the company name, logo and colours for everyone. This can't be undone.

Presets: DQX Default, Databricks, High contrast, Aubergine, Dimmed, Dracula, Nord, Ocean and Solarized.
