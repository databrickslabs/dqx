# Styling

<FeatureTags>
  <FeatureLifecycleStage stage="beta" heading={false} />
  <AvailableSinceVersion version="0.17.0" heading={false} />

Admins can brand DQX Studio for their company under **Settings → Styling**. Changes apply to everyone. Users still choose light or dark mode themselves.

## Company Name

Adds your company to the top bar: **DQX Studio | Acme**. Leave it empty to show **DQX Studio**.

## Custom Logo

Replaces the DQX icon in the top bar. Use one logo for both modes, or separate logos for light and dark mode.

- PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 256 KB. SVG is not supported.
- Logos are shown 24 px tall; wide logos keep their proportions.

## Branding

Pick a preset or set five colours: **Header**, **Page background**, **Text**, **Brand** and **Sidebar**. Studio derives the rest (cards, hover, borders, button text) from them.

- Dark mode matches your light colours automatically. Turn off **Match light mode (auto)** to set dark colours yourself.
- If a colour combination may be hard to read, Studio shows a warning. You can still save.
- Status colours (pass, warn, fail) and chart colours don't change.
- **Reset to DQX Default** removes the company name, logo and colours.

Presets: DQX Default, Databricks, Aubergine, Ocean, Dimmed, High contrast, Nord, Solarized and Dracula.
