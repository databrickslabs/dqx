# Review results

After a [run](/docs/studio/monitoring/run-and-schedule) finishes, its results
show up here — the place to see how your data is doing, find what's failing, and
decide what to do about it. Every monitored table and collection has a
**Results** view.

## Read the score

At the top is the **DQ score** — a 0–100% summary of the latest run. A rule
becomes a **check** when you apply it to a column, and each check produces one
**test** per row when it runs; the score is the **average test pass rate** across
all of them. A **collection** score is the average DQ score across its member
tables. Below the score, breakdowns split the results by **dimension**,
**severity**, **rule**, and **column**, so you can see where quality is slipping.

A check is **flagged as failing** when its pass rate drops below its
[pass threshold](/docs/studio/monitoring/assign-rules#quality-thresholds) (the
`< 70%` badge) — that's separate from the numeric score, which always uses the
raw pass rate.

## Investigate and act on a failure {#investigate-and-act-on-a-failure}

The breakdowns are interactive: select a **dimension**, **severity**, **rule**,
or **column** to filter down to just those results, and on a collection you can
**drill into a member table** to open its own Results and keep going. To see what
actually went wrong, open the **Failed records** table and **hover a failing
cell** — a popup lists every check that row failed, each with its severity and
reason.

A failing check is a prompt to act, not always a problem with the rule. You can
**fix the data at source**, **adjust the threshold** if the target was
unrealistic, **change the severity** to reprioritize, **refine the rule**, or
**escalate** to the team that owns the data. The **Show comments** control opens
a notes thread so you can record what you found and hand it off — each comment
keeps its author and timestamp.

<Admonition type="info" title="Your data is never changed">
Failing a check never deletes or edits your data. The failing rows shown here are
a **sample** copied into a quarantine view purely so you can inspect them.
</Admonition>

## Ask Genie {#ask-genie}

**Ask Genie** turns your results into a conversation. Open the panel on any
Results view and ask a question in plain English — "which columns have the most
failures?", "why did my score drop?" — and Genie answers with a short
explanation and, where it helps, a chart. There's no SQL to write; it's built on
Databricks Genie and reads the same results you're already looking at.

Not sure what to ask? Genie opens with **suggested questions** grouped by what
you're trying to do — Basic stats, Drilldown, Trends over time, and Diagnose.
Every conversation stays with the table or collection you opened it from, and you
can start a **New conversation** or revisit an earlier one from **History** at
any time.
