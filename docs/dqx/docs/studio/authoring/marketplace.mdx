# Marketplace

The **Marketplace** is a catalogue of ready-made, reusable data-quality rules —
so you don't have to write the common ones from scratch. Browse rules grouped by
domain, tick the ones you want, and import them straight into your Rules
Registry as reusable templates you can map onto your tables.

<Admonition type="info" title="Admin only">
The Marketplace is available to workspace **admins**. Find it in the sidebar,
just above **Documentation**.
</Admonition>

## Browse and filter

Rules are grouped into **domain packs** — Contacts & People, Addresses & Geo,
Pricing & Money, Dates & Freshness, Codes & Classifications, Standard checks, and
more. Expand a pack to see its rules; each shows its **quality dimension** and
**severity**.

Two filters narrow what's shown:

- **Industry** — e.g. Banking, Retail, Healthcare, Telco. Rules tagged for that
  industry (plus general rules) show.
- **Region** — e.g. Global, EU, UK, US. Regions are hierarchical: choosing **EU**
  shows every EU rule (including per-country ones like France or Germany), while
  choosing **France** narrows to just that country.

Use the **search** box to find a rule by name across every pack.

## Preview a rule

Click a rule to expand it and see exactly what it does — its logic, parameters,
and whether the row **passes** or **fails** when the condition is true.

### Try it out

Not sure a rule does what you expect? Hit **Try it out** on any rule to open a
test panel. Type a few sample values (or point at one of your tables) and see
per-row pass/fail before you import anything.

## Import rules

Tick the rules you want — individually, or a whole pack via its header
checkbox — then click **Import _N_ selected**. The rules land in your Rules
Registry as reusable templates, ready to [assign to your
tables](/docs/studio/monitoring/assign-rules).

<Admonition type="tip" title="Imported rules arrive approved">
Because Marketplace packs are curated and the Marketplace is admin-only,
imported rules are **published on import** — they don't sit in the approval
queue. Re-importing is safe: a rule you already have is reused, not duplicated.
</Admonition>

## Deploy demo content

The Marketplace is also home to **Deploy demo content** — the first row on the
page. It fills the workspace with example tables, rules, and past run results so
you can explore the Studio with realistic data.

<ThemedImage
  alt="Deploy demo content row at the top of the Marketplace, with a Deploy button"

Click the row, confirm (the default wipes existing Studio data for a clean
slate), and it seeds in the background — it takes a while, so leave it to run.

<Admonition type="note" title="Moved from Settings">
Deploying the demo used to live under **Settings**. It now lives here in the
Marketplace, alongside the rule packs.
</Admonition>

## Contribute a pack

The rule packs are DQX-YAML files in the
[DQX repository](https://github.com/databrickslabs/dqx/tree/main/app/src/databricks_labs_dqx_app/backend/marketplace/packs).
To add a rule or a whole new domain pack, open a pull request — see the README
in that directory for the format. There's a **Contribute on GitHub** link in the
Marketplace header too.
